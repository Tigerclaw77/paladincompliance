# Support: what is offered

- Channel: email to contact@paladincompliance.com. Replies come from the same mailbox.
- Hours and speed: answered on business days (Monday to Friday, U.S. Central time, excluding U.S. federal holidays) on a best-effort basis. There is no guaranteed response time and no service-level agreement unless the owner approves one in writing.
- Scope: how the member workspace works, billing questions, shipping status of a printed binder, correcting a name or address on the registry record, and reports of errors in binder text.
- Out of scope: legal advice, interpreting a law or regulation for a specific situation, completing the member's own policies for them, audits, certification, and regulatory filings.
- Not offered: phone, chat, onboarding calls, or priority support.

The owner must confirm that the contact mailbox actually receives and sends mail before launch (see TEST_EMAIL.md outside this repo). If it does not, set `PALADIN_PUBLIC_CONTACT_EMAIL` to a working address; the site, emails, and legal pages read that setting.
